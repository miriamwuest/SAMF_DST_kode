Download VSCode from https://code.visualstudio.com/

Run the installer (default settings are fine)

Open VSCode

Press Ctrl+Shift+X (or choose Extensions in the left-side Activity bar) 
For Mac: Cmd⌘+Shift+X 

Install Python 

Git:
